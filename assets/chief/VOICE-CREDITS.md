# Living village audio

Three original Japanese encouragement cues were generated for this game on2026-10-08 using the connected Runway text-to-speech tool and existing account credits.

- voice-good.mp3 — 「いい調子！」, Bernard preset, Japanese, speed1.1
- voice-delivery.mp3 — 「納品完了！」, Bernard preset, Japanese, speed1.1
- voice-festival.mp3 — 「大豊作！」, Mark preset, Japanese, speed1.1

No real person's cloned voice was requested. No subscription or credit purchase was made. The existing11 music scores and new wind/water/insect/bird/action sounds are generated by local Web Audio code; they do not require a music streaming service. These cues are embedded in the standalone release, with16-second global voice cooldown and50-second delivery cooldown.

Pixel font remains DotGothic16, with the existing OFL license alongside it. chief-living-pixel.woff2 is a character subset covering the loaded World source/UI strings.
