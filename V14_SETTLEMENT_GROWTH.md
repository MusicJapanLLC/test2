# GUILD∞ v1.4 — Settlement Growth

Owner feedback
- town has too many trees/rocks; outside exploration has no purpose
- walls can be phased through
- enemies arrive too rarely
- huts can appear outside the wall
- walls should expand
- houses and walls need levels that change visuals/effects

Rules
- after palisade exists, the settlement interior is primarily for buildings/people, not respawning resources
- starter resources may exist before the first wall, but depleted/respawned resources relocate outside the wall
- new buildings must choose slots inside current settlement bounds
- player cannot cross wall segments except through the gate while wall HP > 0
- workers route through the gate when their job target is outside
- guards defend from inside the wall until it breaks
- night has multiple waves, not a single tiny spawn
- palisade supports Lv1–Lv4; every expansion enlarges bounds, HP, build slots and visual materials
- huts support Lv1–Lv3; every level changes appearance and increases worker capacity
- no screen shake
- autosave remains mandatory

Acceptance
1. after the wall is built, useful resources are visibly outside the settlement
2. player cannot phase through walls; gate is the valid path
3. huts always build inside current settlement bounds
4. wall expansion immediately makes the town larger
5. Hut Lv1/Lv2/Lv3 look visibly different
6. Palisade Lv1/Lv2/Lv3/Lv4 look visibly different
7. first night produces multiple visible waves
8. workers can leave through the gate, work outside and return/retarget without freezing
