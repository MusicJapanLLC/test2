## What was useful after actually playing the artifact

### Strong reusable interaction patterns
- bottom navigation keeps major systems one tap away
- quest/dispatch loop with visible countdown creates anticipation
- floating +Gold feedback makes rewards feel immediate
- early objective tracker gives a clear next milestone
- adventurer traits are easy to understand when their mechanical effect is stated explicitly
- auto-select is useful for repeat management actions
- facility/upgrades need a visible next action after rewards arrive
- manual save/status feedback improves trust even when auto-save exists

## What NOT to copy
- dense top HUD that mixes rank, reputation and thresholds in one block
- blocking start/title overlay on top of an already-rendered game
- unclear currency labels
- full-screen management for every routine action
- identical early quests with no variation
- crowding the screen with too many systems before the core loop feels good

## How this maps to our v0.8

Now:
- persistent bottom dock
- bottom live ticker
- GUILD UP / HIRE one tap away
- compact tabs for guild/adventurer/quest/facility/record
- drag-only movement

Later, after v0.8 feel is accepted:
- quest countdown cards
- small persistent objective chip
- explicit adventurer traits
- auto-select for repetitive dispatch
- clearer facility progression

Do not build these later systems early if drag feel, typography, bottom dock or core growth loop are still weak.
