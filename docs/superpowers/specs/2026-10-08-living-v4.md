# Living village v4

User explicitly authorizes autonomous substantial redesign and publication on game.music-japan.com. Preserve the title 村長、世界まで行くんですか？ and its pixel typography/world. This is a Mayor World upgrade, not a replacement of other games.

The core promise is a visible, legible village: workers harvest into visible stacks, carry real resources to a depot, deliver to storage, and form contextual relationships; player taps instantly credit resources with expressive, accurate feedback. Every visible number corresponds to actual game state. Production, personality, delivery celebrations, trophies and ambience form one loop rather than isolated menus.

## Systems
Logistics uses stable per-worker cargo and delivered ledgers, bounded capacity, a real depot, unload before night sleep where capacity permits, visible storage/supply/away reasons, fallen cargo recovery, and balanced throughput. Pair relationships use work encounters, deliveries and situational banter; friendship/rivalry have small bounded mood or speed effects and a visible journal. A delivery festival rewards cooperative activity without exponential inflation. Trophy progress is permanent, bounded and claimed at most once. Harvest presentation uses actual decimals, finite combo tiers and readable large text. Living scenery includes wind, leaves, birds, insects, water ripples and night fireflies; layered procedural ambience and short Japanese voice cues support actions with cooldowns. Cross-origin save export/import validates data and keeps a backup; no payment identity export.
